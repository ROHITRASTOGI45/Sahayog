## 📌 About Sahayog

**Sahayog** is a digital platform designed to connect **customers, workers,
and cooperative organizations** through a unified technology-driven ecosystem.

The platform aims to simplify service discovery, worker management,
service requests, bookings, verification, communication and cooperative
administration while creating a more organized and transparent digital
environment for worker cooperatives.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Worker cooperatives and local service workers often face challenges such as:

- Limited digital visibility
- Difficulty finding suitable customers and service opportunities
- Manual worker and service management
- Unorganized booking and service-request processes
- Lack of centralized cooperative administration
- Difficulty matching workers with suitable jobs
- Limited access to data-driven demand insights

Sahayog addresses these challenges through a centralized digital platform.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Sahayog provides separate interfaces and workflows for:

| User | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| 👤 Customer | Discover services, request workers and manage bookings |
| 👷 Worker | Manage profile, availability and service opportunities |
| 🤝 Cooperative | Manage workers, services, requests and operations |

The proposed system combines a modern web interface, backend APIs,
database services, location-based services and AI-assisted matching.

# 🧠 AI-Based Worker Matching

One of the proposed intelligent components of Sahayog is an
**AI-based worker matching system**.

The system can consider parameters such as:

- Worker skills
- Location
- Availability
- Workload
- Service requirements

These parameters can be processed to identify suitable workers
for a particular service requirement.
